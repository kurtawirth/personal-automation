Privacy Policy

Last updated 7 October 2026. Applies to Course Planner (school-agent-506520) and Trivia Night Automation (trivia-night-automation).

Who operates these applications

Both are personal applications operated by Kurt Wirth. They have no users other than their operator and are not distributed to anyone else.

Whose data is accessed

Only the operator's own Google account data. These applications do not request, receive, or store data belonging to any other person. No account other than the operator's has ever been authorized, and the applications provide no mechanism for anyone else to sign in.

What is accessed, and why

  • Google Drive, Docs, and Slides — to read the operator's own syllabi, course notes, and trivia materials, and to create and edit documents and presentations in the operator's own Drive.
  • Gmail (send and modify) — used only by Trivia Night Automation, to send scheduling email on the operator's behalf and to read replies to it.

Where information is stored

Access credentials and any retrieved content remain on hardware the operator owns: a personal desktop computer and a Raspberry Pi on a home network. Nothing is stored on a hosted server, and no database is exposed to the internet.

What is shared

Nothing. Data is not sold, rented, published, or transferred to any third party. The only external services contacted are Google's own APIs, and Anthropic's API for generating document text. No human other than the operator reviews the content.

Limited use

Use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Retention and deletion

Because the only data involved belongs to the operator, it is retained for as long as the operator keeps it. Access can be withdrawn at any time from Google Account permissions, which immediately revokes the stored credentials and stops all access.

Changes

If these applications change what they access, this page will be updated before that change takes effect.

Contact

Questions can be raised at github.com/kurtawirth/personal-automation/issues.
