Personal Automation

Two small private applications operated by Kurt Wirth.

The applications described on this page are personal tools. They are run by one person, on that person's own computers, against that person's own Google account. They are not offered as a product or service, have no users other than their operator, and collect no data from anyone else.

Course Planner

school-agent-506520

Prepares weekly teaching materials — lesson plans, slide decks, and reading notes — for university communication courses. It reads the operator's own syllabi and course notes from Google Drive and writes new documents and presentations back to the same Drive.

Trivia Night Automation

trivia-night-automation

Builds and formats question decks for a weekly pub trivia night, and sends and reads scheduling email on the operator's behalf.
